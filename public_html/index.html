About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Facebook AI Research (FAIR), working with Yann LeCun.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from MIT, advised by professor Marin Soljačić in 2019.
I co-founded Lightelligence Inc. in 2017.
I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014.

My current research interest is in self-supervised learning.
