Profile

Li Jing is a senior year Ph.D. student in MIT physics department. He works in Marin soljačić's group. His research interest includes Deep Learning and Applied Physics.
Li Jing received B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014.
Li Jing won 2010 International Physics Olympiad Gold (IPhO) medal representing China.
