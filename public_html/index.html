About

Li Jing is a postdoctoral researcher at Facebook AI Research (FAIR), working with Yann LeCun on self-supervised learning. His research interests span a wide range of core AI areas and applications, including representation learning, optimization, computer vision, natural language processing, AI for science. Before joining FAIR, Li obtained his PhD in physics at MIT, advised by professor Marin Soljacic. Li received a BS in physics and a BA in economics from Peking University. Li is also a co-founder of Lightelligence Inc., which builds optical AI computing chips. Li has been awarded Forbes China 30 under 30, in the domain of enterprise technology. Li has won a gold medal in the 2010 International Physics Olympiad (IPhO).
