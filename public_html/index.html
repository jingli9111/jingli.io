About

I am a 5th year Ph.D. student at MIT, working with professor Marin soljačić. I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014. I co-founded Lightelligence Inc. in 2017.

My research interests include Recurrent Neural Networks, Natural Language Processing, Sparse and Efficient Models, Deep Learning for Physics.
