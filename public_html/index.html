About

I am a senior Ph.D. student at MIT, advised by professor Marin soljačić. My research interests focus on Deep Learning and Applied Physics.
I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014.
I won 2010 International Physics Olympiad (IPhO) Gold Medal representing China.

I co-founded Lightelligence Inc.. By processing information with light, our AI accelarators offer orders of magnitude improvement in terms of high speed, low latency, and low power consumption compared to traditional electronic architectures.
