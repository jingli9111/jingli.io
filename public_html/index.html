About

I am a 5th Ph.D. student at MIT, working with professor Marin soljačić. My research interests include Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing and Applied Physics. I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014.

I am a co-founder of Lightelligence Inc. where I led the Machine Learning Algorithm team. We raised $10.7M seed-series funding in 2017.
