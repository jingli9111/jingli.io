About

I am a 6th year physics Ph.D. student at MIT, working with professor Marin soljačić. My research interest is Machine Learning in general, including Recurrent Neural Networks, Natural Language Processing, Reinforcement Leraning, Neural Network Compression, Deep Learning for Physics.

I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics (Dual Major) from Peking University in 2014.

I co-founded Lightelligence Inc. in 2017.

I won a gold medal at 2010 International Physics Olympiad (IPhO). I was honored 2019 Forbes China 30 under 30 (Enterprise Technology).

[2019/12] I will defend my thesis on 12/16.
