I am a 3rd year Ph.D. student in MIT physics department. I work as a research assistant in Professor Marin soljačić's group. My research interest includes Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning and Optics.

My research can be summarized as two main directions:

  • Use physics ideas to design better neural networks as well as to deeply understand neural networks.
  • Use AI to design novel physics applications

I also have experience in web development(both front-end and back-end), iOS development and quantitive trading. I am happy to work with any interesting project that can have a strong impact and long term benefit.

Feel free to email me if you want to collaborate.
